## 📐 Our Standards

Every line we ship is held to these principles:

- 🔒 **Secure enough for anyone** — we build products we'd trust with our own money and data. E2EE, hashed OTPs, hardened servers — security is the default, not the upsell.
- 🇰🇪 **East Africa-native** — M-Pesa flows, eTIMS compliance, SMS-first UX, and connectivity realities baked in from day one, not bolted on.
- ⚡ **Performance-first** — we optimise before it's a problem, not after.
- 📈 **Scalable by design** — every architecture decision is made with growth in mind, and every infrastructure shilling is justified by revenue.
- 🤖 **AI-ready** — systems designed so automation and intelligent workflows plug in cleanly.
- 🎯 **Owned end-to-end** — we don't hand off half-built work. We see it through to production and keep it running.
